# Surface Brief: MyCCS Mobile Journey Prototype (Student + Org Officer)

- **Target:** `docs/user-journey/demo/index.html`
- **Mode:** `Operate`
- **Audience:** CCS Students (`student` role, 9 screens) and Organization Officers (`officer` role, 5 screens), reviewed by product/engineering stakeholders to establish `DESIGN.md`.
- **Job:** Walk through all 14 MVP mobile screens end-to-end with interactive state transitions (OTP input, conditional `visibleIf` membership form, QR lanyard flip + max brightness, masked GMetrix key reveal/copy, onsite vs online event creation, offline QR door scanner simulation, applicant approve/reject, evaluation metrics) and inspect the visual system.
- **Constraints:** Sample data clearly labeled as demo data; RA 10173 privacy notices preserved; zero PII in QR payloads; read-only org posts (no likes/comments); 44px+ touch targets; high contrast for bright hallways and dim auditoriums.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** Translate the official CCS department crest (Deep Crest Navy, Circuit Royal Blue, Shield Cyan, Laurel Gold) into an editorial lanyard & academic ledger utility—paired with stacked color-block hero decks and full-pill CTAs—built for bright hallways and zero-connectivity auditorium doors.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Cool crest canvas (`#F6F9FD`), elevated crisp white ruled sheets (`#FFFFFF`), slate secondary wells (`#EDF2F9`), hairline cool rules (`#DCE5F2`), navy-graphite ink (`#0B192C`), secondary slate (`#415166`), Deep Crest Navy primary pill CTAs (`#0B2E59`), Circuit Royal Blue active/hover (`#0052CC`), Shield Cyan deck/scanner accent (`#0EA5E9`), Laurel Gold graphic/icon/peek accent (`#D4A500`, AA-safe `#856404` on `#FEF9E2` for text badges), Present Emerald (`#067647`), Absent Crimson (`#B42318`), and Auditorium Navy (`#0B2545`) reserved strictly for the offline door scanner and lanyard header band. Typography uses `Public Sans` for UI/headings and `JetBrains Mono` strictly for OTP digits, signed QR tokens, clock offsets, and masked GMetrix keys.
- **STORY:** A stakeholder or student immediately recognizes MyCCS as an official College of Information Technology and Computer Science (IT, CS, and LIS) utility, switches seamlessly between all 9 Student screens and 5 Org Officer screens (or steps through each journey in sequence), tests real interactive controls (conditional forms, QR lanyard flip, offline scan states, masked key reveal), and sees a single coherent design system ready to codify into `DESIGN.md`.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** On desktop, a calm studio shell frames a true 390×844 mobile viewport centered alongside a compact left journey rail (design variation switcher, Student 9 screens / Org Officer 5 screens, and Prev/Next stepper) and a concise right screen reference strip showing the active screen's FR code, active variation description, and interactive hints. On mobile viewports (`<= 768px`), the phone screen fills the viewport directly with a slim top persona/screen switcher bar so the prototype tests natively at 390px width. Signature interaction: live role & screen switching with stateful interactive controls (physical Y-axis 3D card flip on Student 7 My QR ID, dynamic `visibleIf` reveal on Student 5 & 9, live offline scan simulator on Officer 3, and reveal/copy toggle on Student 8 My Keys).
- **FORM:** Campus Lanyard & Passbook (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, candidate #1 on our ordered grounded list; seed key `85a445d9`).
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
